Public opinion vs lived experience

How accepting is public opinion across Europe? Does public acceptance translate into everyday experiences?

Here is what people think is happening (Eurobarometer), next to what LGBTQ+ people actually report living through (FRA Survey III). For each country, the first bar shows discrimination reported by LGBTQ+ people in 2023 across everyday life, employment, healthcare, and education, while the second bar reflects broader public opinion on LGBTQ+ rights and issues.

Side-by-side, these datasets reveal the gaps between societal perception and daily reality across the European Union.

How to use: Select a topic (discrimination in everyday life, employment, healthcare, or education), then choose one of the corresponding public opinion questions to compare.

Discrimination Felt

From Laws to Lives: Rainbow Families across Europe

This map traces the legal recognition of same-sex and different-sex couples regarding marriage, registered partnerships, cohabitation, and adoption.

The visualization draws from two distinct sources: the Laws and Families dataset, which tracks 20 European countries from 1989 to 2016, and Rainbow Europe, which reflects the current legal landscape in 2024. The remaining gap (2016–2024) highlights a critical area for future legal research and updates.

How to use: Move the slider on the left to see how legislation changed through the years, click on a country to have all the details.
